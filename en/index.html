Cubos is a two-year course to learn to solve the cube, for iPhone and iPad, in ten languages. Every turn is animated in 3D, and it has a competition timer, an algorithm trainer and a camera solver. The first month is free; the full course is unlocked with a single payment.

Frequently asked questions

Which cube do I need?

Any 3x3 cube with the standard colours (white opposite yellow, green opposite blue, red opposite orange). A magnetic speed cube, for 10 to 20 euros, turns far better than a toy one.

The scanner misreads a colour

Find even light without reflections, and fill the frame with the face. After scanning you can fix any sticker by tapping it. Red and orange are the easiest to mix up in low light.

It says my cube cannot be solved

Usually a sticker was misread. Otherwise someone twisted a corner by hand or took the cube apart and put it back wrong: take that piece out and put it back correctly.

I changed iPhone and cannot see my purchase

Go to Today › Settings (the gear) › Restore purchase, with the same Apple Account you bought with. The purchase can be shared with Family Sharing.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. The app has no accounts or servers. If you delete the app, the progress is lost.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole course, the algorithms, the timer and the scanner work offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore.
