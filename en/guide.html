How to solve a Rubik's cube step by step (beginner's method)

Notation, the seven steps of the layer-by-layer method and how to move on to CFOP to get faster.

Before you turn: pieces and notation

The cube has centres (they never move relative to each other and set each face’s colour), edges (two colours) and corners (three colours). Turns are written with letters: R (right), L (left), U (up), D (down), F (front) and B (back); a prime (R′) is counter-clockwise and a 2 (R2) is a half turn.

The seven layer-by-layer steps

1. The white cross, each edge next to its centre. 2. The white corners: first layer done. 3. The middle-layer edges. 4. The yellow cross on top. 5. Placing the yellow edges. 6. Placing the yellow corners. 7. Orienting the corners. Each step uses one or two short sequences you repeat.

How to learn it without stress

One step per session, and do not move on until you can do it without looking. Repeat the sequence with your eyes closed: your fingers remember it before your head does. In a week or two you solve the whole cube from memory.

If you get stuck

It is almost always step 3 or step 7. In step 7 it is normal to feel you are undoing everything: keep turning only the top layer between corners and the cube comes back together.

Next: getting faster

The next jump is CFOP: planning the cross during inspection, intuitive F2L (first and second layers together), then two-look and full OLL and PLL. With fingertricks and look-ahead you go from a minute to under 20 seconds.

How Cubos helps

Cubos teaches this method with every turn animated in 3D and, if you get stuck, scans your cube with the camera and takes you to the end with the same steps. It includes a WCA timer and an algorithm trainer. The beginner’s method is free.
