Last updated: 5 November 2026

In one sentence: Cubos collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and sends nothing to any server of mine, because there is no server of mine.

What is stored and where

The app stores on your device what it needs to work: the lessons you have done, your timer results and their scrambles, the algorithms you are learning, your practice minutes and your daily goal. That file never leaves your device.

Camera

If you use the scanner, the app reads the colours of your cube with the camera. Each frame is analysed on your device on the spot and discarded: nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. If you do not allow the camera, you can enter the colours by hand.

Purchases

The full-course purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no data about you or your payment method.

What there isn't

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking.
  • No third-party components that collect data.

Crash reports

If you have "Share With App Developers" turned on, Apple may send me anonymous technical crash reports. Apple handles them, and you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

As the app collects no personal data, it collects no data from children either.
